## Poster and interface artwork

The geometric poster collage, seat-map thumbnail, pass pattern, neutral image
placeholder, and empty-state icons are original vector/code artwork authored
for this application. No stock illustration or AI-generated photo is used.
The supplied Events mark remains the source of the brand symbol.

## Wordmark

The Events wordmark is outlined from Inter Medium, optical size 14, at 48 units
with -0.5 units letter spacing. Font source:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter
The original font license is preserved in `licenses/Inter-OFL.txt`.
The rendered wordmark contains paths and does not require a font installation.

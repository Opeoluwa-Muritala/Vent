# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform for Android, iOS, tablet, and web; Ktor and PostgreSQL on Railway.

## Users

Attendees check in, find seats, answer polls, and track rewards. Organizers configure and operate events. Staff perform controlled fallback check-in for an assigned hall.

## Product Purpose

Vent runs an internal event from setup through a personal post-event recap while keeping seating, check-in, and engagement in one trusted workflow.

## Positioning

Vent turns the real geometry of a venue into the shared operating surface for attendees and event teams.

## Capabilities and Constraints

One venue, theatre and grid templates, versioned polling rather than WebSockets, Android camera scanning, manual token fallback on iOS and web, no payments, ticket marketplace, push notifications, or offline mode.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Vent. The visual system is independent and has no Google or GDG affiliation. Each event supplies one seed color from which accessible semantic schemes are derived.

## Product Principles

- Make the next event action obvious.
- Let venue geometry communicate location and progress.
- Fail closed for identity, role, booking, and check-in decisions.
- Make state understandable without relying on color.
- Keep mobile operation fast while expanding naturally on larger screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, non-color state cues, large touch targets, screen-reader labels, platform font scaling, reduced motion, and accessible-seat validation are required.
